## Figures not to repeat as fact

These are Martell's own statements, unverified, and some appear in promotional videos. Never use them on calls, in emails or in copy as evidence.

- 92% of work across his companies done by AI agents (V2)
- 47 agents in 8 weeks (V1)
- $70,000 a year saved by one prompt (V1)
- An airline support agent falling from 33% to 11% success (V2)
- 170 million new jobs by 2030 (V2)
- Referrals raising sales by 30% (V4)
- A team member spending 10 hours a day on her inbox (V1)
- AI "doesn't make mistakes" (V7). His own story in V1 shows otherwise.

V1 also promotes one specific product, Grok Bot, and his own paid offers. Treat tool recommendations with care, and note that some details in V1 may change quickly.
